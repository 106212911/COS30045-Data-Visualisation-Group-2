Road Crash Hospitalisations in Australia

Exploring hospitalised road injuries from 2011 to 2021

National Hospitalisation Trends

How have road crash hospitalisations in Australia changed between 2011 and 2021?

Key finding: National road crash hospitalisations generally increased between 2011 and 2019. They declined by approximately 4.8% in 2020 before rising again in 2021. These figures represent hospitalisation counts, rather than population-adjusted injury rates.

First Nations Hospitalisation Trends

How have hospitalisations changed for First Nations peoples compared with Non-Indigenous Australians?

Key finding: First Nations hospitalisations rose from 1,167 in 2011 to 2,220 in 2021 (about 90%), compared with about 15% for Non-Indigenous Australians. They also rose by about 23% in 2020, when national hospitalisations fell. These figures are counts, not population-adjusted rates, so use the growth view to compare the two groups fairly.
