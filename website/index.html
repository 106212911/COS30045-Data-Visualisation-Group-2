Road Crash Hospitalisations in Australia

Exploring hospitalised road injuries from 2011 to 2021

National Hospitalisation Trends

How have road crash hospitalisations in Australia changed between 2011 and 2021?

Key finding: National road crash hospitalisations generally increased between 2011 and 2019. They declined by approximately 4.8% in 2020 before rising again in 2021. These figures represent hospitalisation counts, rather than population-adjusted injury rates.

Data Source: BITRE (2023)
