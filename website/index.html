National Hospitalisation Trends

How have road crash hospitalisations in Australia changed between 2011 and 2021?

Key finding: Hospitalisations increased from 34,033 in 2011 to 39,505 in 2021. They declined by approximately 4.8% in 2020 before rising again in 2021.
